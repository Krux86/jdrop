JDrop Settings

When a link or Click'N'Load capture comes in, it's sent immediately to your last-used device with your last-used options - the review panel won't open at all. Off by default so you stay in control of what gets sent; turn this on once you trust the defaults and just want things to go through without a click.

When you copy a link, or a list of links (one per line), it's added to the review panel automatically - the same panel used for right-click and CNL. This always goes through the panel and is never sent automatically, even if auto-send above is also on, since copying something is a much more incidental action than an explicit click. Off by default. Detection happens at the moment you press copy by checking what's selected on the page - it never reads your system clipboard directly, so no extra permission is needed.
